# Gate G3 — garment separation

**Scope:** tasks T30–T38.

## Checklist

1. For every sample, each visible garment appears once in `s06_garments/`
   with the right category and strategy. Missing or duplicated items are listed.
2. `skin_layer` garments: no gaps at seams, offset ≤ 3 mm, texture boundary
   matches the image (neckline, cuffs, leg openings).
3. `template` garments: the chosen template is a sensible match; fit has no
   visible clipping in A-pose; colours and patterns match the image.
4. `generated` garments: watertight or deliberately open, < 50k triangles at
   LOD0, cleaned of floaters.
5. Textures: base colour, normal, roughness (and metallic where needed) at
   the configured resolution; no obvious seams or smears on the visible front;
   back side plausible.
6. Assembly: all garments skinned to the body skeleton; a test walk animation
   in Blender (T36) shows no major clipping.
7. UE5 screenshots from the owner: garments can be hidden and shown one by one.
8. Budget check.

- Rewrite M4 cards (T40–T45).
